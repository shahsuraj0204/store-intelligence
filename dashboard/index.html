The Challenge

Retail Visibility Bottlenecks

Traditional stores suffer from blindspots in shopper behavior.

Blind Dwell Times: High traffic shelves but unknown drop-off factors.
Checkout Abandonment: Long queues build up without predictive staff allocation.
Static Store Layouts: No correlation between item purchase affinity and physical traffic.
Technical Stack

Edge-to-Cloud System Flow

A real-time, lightweight, database-backed processing pipeline.

CV Pipeline: YOLOv8 + ByteTrack running frame-skipped inferences on CPU.
SQLite & FastAPI: Dynamic database storage storing trajectories and POS tables.
WebSockets: Real-time asynchronous event broadcasts straight to frontend.
Core Innovation 1

Spatial Re-ID Journey Timeline

Tracing exact multi-camera transitions for individual customer sessions.

Reconstructs paths from entrance, shelf zones, queues, and checkout.
Generates detailed telemetry: visit duration, dwell time, and speed.
Classifies shopper profiles and segmenting employee traffic automatically.
Core Innovation 2

POS Basket Correlation A/B

Mining POS baskets and mapping them to physical customer paths.

POS Affinity: Association rules showing buy-together category strengths.
CV Flow: Tracks physical transition rate between shelves.
PEI Score: Identifies layout bottlenecks (high affinity + low flow).
Core Innovation 3

Interactive Voice & Sound Copilot

An eyes-free, voice-assistant desk for busy store managers.

Web Audio Synthesizer: Clean sound design generated in-browser.
TTS Announcements: Dynamic voice narration of alerts, queues, and insights.
Auditory Alarms: Critical sound flags for restricted area intrusions.
