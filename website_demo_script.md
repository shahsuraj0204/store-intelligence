# Apex Retail - Website Demo Walkthrough Script

This script is for a **1 to 1.5-minute demo** where you only show the live dashboard website in action.

---

### Step 1: Landing Page & Starting Simulation
* **Action**: Open `http://localhost:8000/dashboard` in fullscreen mode (`F11`). Point to the clean dark-mode interface, then click the **"Run Simulation"** button in the top right.
* **What to Say**:
  > "Welcome to the Apex Retail Store Intelligence Desk. This is the main live operational dashboard. 
  > 
  > I'll start by clicking the 'Run Simulation' button here. This simulates real-time shopper telemetry streaming from my in-store cameras. 
  > Instantly, you see my live event ticker on the bottom right updating with entries, exits, and queue events as they happen."

---

### Step 2: KPI Metrics & Funnel
* **Duration**: ~20 seconds
* **Action**: Hover your cursor over the four KPI cards (Unique Visitors, Conversion Rate, Queue Depth, Queue Abandonment) at the top, then scroll down to the **Customer Conversion Funnel** chart.
* **What to Say**:
  > "At the top, my KPI cards update dynamically. I monitor unique shoppers, purchase conversion, and active cashier queue depths. 
  > Below, the conversion funnel visualizes how shoppers transition from the entrance doors, to browsing product aisles, joining checkout queues, and completing their transactions."

---

### Step 3: Store Heatmap & Visitor Journeys
* **Duration**: ~20 seconds
* **Action**: Scroll down to show the **Product Zone Heatmap** (with colored zones) and the **Shopper Journey Re-ID Tracker**. Click on one of the active visitor cards on the left side of the journey panel to reveal their step-by-step path timeline.
* **What to Say**:
  > "My store layout heatmap visually displays visit frequency and dwell times across product zones like Skincare and Makeup. 
  > On the Re-ID tracker, I can click on individual visitor IDs to trace their exact sequence of movement from camera to camera, separating employee paths from customer journeys."

---

### Step 4: Layout Optimizer & AI Copilot Alerts
* **Duration**: ~20 seconds
* **Action**: Scroll down to show the **Layout Placement Optimizer** cards. Then scroll back to the top to highlight the **AI Operations Copilot** and the **Security Alerts Desk**.
* **What to Say**:
  > "The layout optimizer matches physical shelf visits with POS basket transactions to recommend shelf updates. 
  > Meanwhile, my AI Copilot displays real-time actions, and the alerts desk flags issues like long queue depths or unauthorized entries. 
  > It's a complete, real-time analytics desk for brick-and-mortar stores. Thank you!"
